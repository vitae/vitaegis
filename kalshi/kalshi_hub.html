How the PowerShell bot works

  1. Find the market. Every 15 seconds it asks Kalshi for the BTC 15-minute market closing soonest. Each one asks: will BTC be at or above the target when the 15 minutes end?
  2. Watch prices live. With your API key it streams every YES/NO price change over a WebSocket ("streaming" above), so it reacts within milliseconds.
  3. Check the rules on every price change. In order: already traded this market? Daily trade cap? Daily loss stop? Too late in the window? Is either side priced inside the buy zone? The first rule that fails is the reason shown in the signal panel.
  4. Size the trade. It buys just enough contracts that a win nets about the win target after Kalshi's fee, but never risks more than the max per trade.
  5. Place the order. In live mode it sends one immediate-or-cancel order at that price: it fills right away or not at all. Test mode only logs what it would have bought.
  6. Settle. About two minutes after a market closes, it fetches the result, records the profit or loss, and counts it toward the daily loss stop.

Results over the last 300 markets (about 3 days): the current setting (favorite at 65–80¢, first 5 minutes) netted −$3.52 over 254 trades with 72% won; it made money in one half of the period and lost in the other. Wins are frequent but small, and losses are bigger. No setting tested showed a reliable edge, so keep stakes small and watch the P&L.
